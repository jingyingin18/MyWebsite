I am Jingying Wang, a researcher with training in psychology and an interdisciplinary research trajectory centered on neuropsychiatric disorders. 

I received my Ph.D. from the Institute of Psychology, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, in 2017. where my work began with modeling openly observable social network behavior, then expanded into identifying affective disorders from visible behavioral signals such as voice and gait.

I have accumulated extensive postdoctoral research experience focusing on neuropsychiatric disorders over five and a half years, with additional work involving wearable devices, EEG, brain stimulation, and neuroimaging technologies.

During the subsequent transition period, I remained actively engaged in academic activities, including publishing manuscripts, peer reviewing, and contributing to ongoing research.
