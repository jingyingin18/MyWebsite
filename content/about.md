## Biography

I am a researcher with training in psychology and an interdisciplinary research trajectory centered on **neuropsychiatric disorders**.

My scientific path began with the study of publicly observable behavior in social networks. The modeling experience I developed there later became foundational for research on identifying mood disorders from visible behavioral signals, including voice and gait.

During my postdoctoral period, my work expanded to include:

- quantitative assessment of Parkinsonian symptoms using wearable and wireless sensors,
- the effects of noninvasive brain stimulation on brain function, and
- neuroimaging approaches to aging in Autism.

These experiences strengthened my methodological foundation for studying symptom expression and brain change together. More broadly, they prepared me to investigate how behavioral phenotypes, neural mechanisms, and disease progression interact across a range of neuropsychiatric conditions.

## Research Interests

- Analysis and prediction
- Brain stimulation
- Wearable and wireless sensing
- Aging and neuroimaging
